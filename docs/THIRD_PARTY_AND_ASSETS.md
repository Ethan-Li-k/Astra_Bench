# Third-party provenance and assets

Existing selected sources in `GPT-as-Policy/`, `ref/GPT-as-Policy/` and `ref/RoboRSI/` retain their LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE files and reference/build documentation. They are local copies already present in RSI; source hashes identify the captured bytes. Nested `.git` metadata is excluded, so no new upstream revision verification is claimed.

The GPT-as-Policy copy includes its LICENSE, THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES and licenses subtree. RoboRSI and its nested components retain their own notices. Each component's existing license applies to that component; it is not automatically a license for RSI additions. No first-party license is added or changed by this snapshot.

SIMPLE, Loco_manip/locomanip, model_38000 weights, simulator environments and the original external IKEA assets remain dependencies. External directories and symlink targets were not copied. Locally retained large data, media, recordings, binary assets, installed compatibility packages and weights remain excluded. A source path does not grant redistribution rights for its external target.

Eight previously withheld peripheral helper/test files remain excluded under the existing scan decision; they were not reread or re-researched in the final increment. The exact excluded paths and categories are in the companion exclusion manifest. All original source/data remains in RSI, and primary source coverage is independently checked.
